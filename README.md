### Facet — On-Device Computer Vision and 3D Interaction

## Research

My undergraduate research focused on wildfire and smoke localization and classification using deep learning. The work included:

- Curating and manually annotating a **2,843-image** object-detection dataset
- Modifying YOLOv8s with **Mish activation functions** and **SPD-Conv**
- Extending the dataset with polygon masks using **SAM 2 Large**
- Benchmarking preprocessing, inference, and postprocessing latency on Apple Silicon

The resulting paper, **“Mish-Activated YOLOv8s for Wildfire and Smoke Detection,”** was presented at **IEEE COMPAS 2026** in Dhaka. The paper was co-authored with my undergraduate thesis supervisor, Maisha Fahmida.
